The small print

What you are buying

A Shitebox is a mystery box. We choose what goes in it and you do not. The contents are random, low in value and different every time. Photos and descriptions on this site show the idea, not your box.

Second-hand items

Around 40% of what we send is bought from charity shops, so some items in every box are second-hand.

Nothing that could be a hygiene risk is ever second-hand. Everything is checked and sent intact and safe, if not useful.

The charities

We buy stock from charity shops, including British Heart Foundation, Mind, YMCA, Oxfam and St Barnabas. The money we spend in their shops is how your order supports them.

We are not partnered with these charities and they have not endorsed us. We do not promise a fixed donation per box.

Making it personal

Telling us about the person getting the box is optional. We use what you tell us to write the jokes for that box, and for nothing else.

Changing your mind

You can cancel for any reason up to 14 days after your box is delivered. Tell us, send it back, and we refund you.

If something is wrong

If an item arrives broken or the box is damaged, tell us and we will put it right. Disappointing is the product. Broken is not.

Who can buy

Boxes are sold to adults. They are gifts for adults, not toys.

Nothing in this small print affects your statutory rights.
