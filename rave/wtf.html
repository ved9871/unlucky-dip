WTF is Shite­box?

A mystery box of presents nobody asked for, from people who can't be arsed to try.

Buying a present is a gamble you keep losing. We've removed the gamble by removing the choice. You pick a size. We fill it with upcycled shite, mundane shite and novelty shite. Whoever opens it gets what they're given, and has nobody to blame but us.

We're nifty at being thrifty! A good chunk of every box is upcycled. We go round the charity shops with a budget and no taste, and the money we spend stays with the charity. So the worse our shopping gets, the more good it does. We're quite proud of that. It's the only thing.

We're pranksters, not a gift shop. Every single box is different, every single box is a surprise, and we will not be telling you what's inside.

Where we shop

The charities whose shops we raid. We upcycle the shite, they keep the money.

We buy from their shops like anyone else. They haven't endorsed us, and frankly who would...

How it works

Three steps. You do one of them.

  1. You pick a size

    Little or Big. Make it a Ninja Box if they mustn't see it coming. That's your whole job.

  2. We fill it

    Upcycled shite that deserved a second chance, plus the mundane and the novelty.

  3. They open it

    You film their face. They scan the box. We explain ourselves.

The small print. Some items in every box are second-hand, upcycled from charity shops. Nothing that could be a hygiene risk is second-hand. Contents are random and low in value; you are paying for the laugh. None of this affects your statutory rights. Read all of the small print.
