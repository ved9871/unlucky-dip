The small print

What you are buying

A Shitebox is a mystery box. We choose what goes in it and you do not. The contents are random, low in value and different every time. Photos and descriptions on this site show the idea, not your box.

Second-hand and upcycled items

Around 60% of the items we send are upcycled: bought from charity shops and given a second go. So some items in every box are second-hand.

Nothing that could be a hygiene risk is ever second-hand. Everything is checked and sent intact and safe, if not useful.

The charities

We buy stock from charity shops, including British Heart Foundation, Mind, YMCA, Oxfam and St Barnabas. The money we spend in their shops is how your order supports them.

We are not partnered with these charities and they have not endorsed us. We do not promise a fixed donation per box.

Postage

UK postage is £4.70 a box, sent Royal Mail tracked, and is added to the price at the checkout.

Ninja Box

Boxes are branded on the outside as standard. A Ninja Box is sent in a plain box with no Shitebox or unluckydip.com branding on the outside. The contents are the same and it costs no more.

Making it personal

Telling us about the person getting the box is optional. We use what you tell us to write the jokes for that box, and for nothing else.

Disappointing is not a fault

The contents are meant to be rubbish. Not liking what you were given does not make an item faulty.

Your right to cancel

The law gives you 14 days from delivery to cancel an online order. To use it, tell us, then send the whole box back at your own cost. We refund you when it arrives.

Boxes we have personalised for you are made to order and cannot be cancelled once we have started packing them.

If something is wrong

If an item arrives broken or the box is damaged, tell us and we will put it right. Disappointing is the product. Broken is not.

Who can buy

Boxes are sold to adults. They are gifts for adults, not toys.

Nothing in this small print affects your statutory rights.
