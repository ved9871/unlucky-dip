FAQ. Unfortunately.

What do you mean, upcycled?

Around 60% of what goes in a box is rescued from charity shops and given one more chance to disappoint. So yes, some items have had a previous life. Nothing that could be a hygiene risk is ever second-hand.

What is inside?

Stuff. Mostly. Some upcycled shite, some mundane shite and some novelty shite. Random, low-value goods. You are paying for the laugh, and we say so before you pay.

Can I make it personal?

Yes. When you order we ask who it's for, what they're shite at and how harsh we can be, and we aim the insults. Or hit Randomise and take pot luck.

I'm out of gift ideas.

Good. Stop having them. Gift ideas are how you ended up buying your dad socks again. You pick a size, we take the decision, and they get what they're given. If it's rubbish, just blame us.

What is a Ninja Box?

The same shite in a plain box, with our name nowhere on the outside, at no extra cost. They won't know what it is until it's open. Meet the Ninja Box.

What is Box Scan?

Every box has a place to scan. Scan it once the box is open and we explain ourselves, item by item. Box Scan is here.

Which charities?

We shop at British Heart Foundation, Mind, YMCA, Oxfam and St Barnabas, and we're adding more. We buy from their shops like anyone else. They haven't endorsed us, and frankly who would...

It's shite. Can I have my money back?

It's meant to be shite. That's not a fault, that's the product, and you get what you're given. If something turns up broken, tell us and we put it right. Your legal rights are set out in the small print.
