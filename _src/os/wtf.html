WTF is Shitebox?

A mystery box of presents nobody asked for, for people who can't be arsed to choose one.

Buying a present is a gamble you keep losing. We've removed the gamble by removing the choice. You pick a size. We fill it with some mundane shite and some novelty shite. Whoever opens it has nobody to blame but us.

A good chunk of it comes from charity shops. We go round them with a budget and no taste, and the money we spend stays with the charity. So the worse our shopping gets, the more good it does. We're quite proud of that. It's the only thing.

We're pranksters, not a gift shop. Every box is different, every box is a surprise, and we will not be telling you what's in yours.
