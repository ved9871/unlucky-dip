Enquiries

FAQ. Unfortunately.

What is inside?

Stuff. Mostly. Some mundane shite and some novelty shite: three or four items in the Little Shite, five to seven in the Big Shite. Random, low-value goods. You are paying for the laugh, and we say so before you pay.

Is any of it second-hand?

Yes. Around 40% of what we send is rescued from charity shops, so some items have had a previous life. Nothing that could be a hygiene risk is ever second-hand.

I don't know what to get someone.

Then stop. That's the whole idea. You pick a size, we take the decision, and if it's rubbish you tell them to blame us.

Can I make it personal?

Yes. Tell us their name, what they're shite at and how harsh we can be, out of 10. We aim the insults accordingly. Or tell us nothing and take pot luck.

Will they know what it is before they open it?

Not if you choose incognito mode. The box goes out with nothing on the outside to give it away.

Which charities?

So far we shop at British Heart Foundation, Mind, YMCA, Oxfam and St Barnabas. We buy from their shops like anyone else. They haven't endorsed us, and frankly who would.

Can I return it?

Yes. You have 14 days from delivery to change your mind, and anything faulty or damaged is put right. This does not affect your statutory rights.
